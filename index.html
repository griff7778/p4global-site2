10000 OTR — High Gas-Permeability Film

Our best-selling bag/liner series, purpose-built for fresh seafood and other controlled-atmosphere applications that need a very high oxygen transmission rate. Runs 50 microns in bag form (70 microns for VSP), rated at OTR 10,000 cc/m²/day, with the clarity and tear-resistant seal strength buyers expect from our protein-grade lines.
